The format

Everything that happens
between joining and ranking.

01 — The room

The battle doesn't start until a hundred people are in it.

You pick a category, the lobby fills, and everybody gets the same fifty questions in the same order at the same moment.

Five rounds
02 — Clock

15s

Per question. Speed is scored alongside accuracy.

03 — Length

50

Questions per battle, split across five rounds.

04 — Correct

+2

XP for a right answer. Wrong answers subtract one.

05 — Floor

0

A battle never leaves you with less XP than you started.

06 — Lifelines

Three per battle. 50/50 removes two wrong answers, Skip passes without penalty, +Time adds seconds to the clock.

07 — Verification

Answers are checked on our servers, not on your phone. Your final rank is calculated server-side against everyone in the room.

The reward system

Two currencies.
They never convert into each other.

This trips people up, so here it is plainly: playing battles earns XP. Coins come from somewhere else entirely.

Currency one

XP

Earned by playing battles. XP sets your rank, your leaderboard position, and part of your withdrawal eligibility.

  • ✦+2 XP per correct answer, −1 per wrong one
  • ✦Net XP per battle is never negative
  • ✦Daily earning is capped; lifetime total never resets
  • ✦No cash value. Cannot be withdrawn.
Currency two

Coins

Earned by completing offer tasks from our partners, and by referring people who go on to play.

  • ✦Surveys, app trials, and partner tasks in the Earn tab
  • ✦Coins for each referral that meets the verification criteria
  • ✦Withdrawable once every eligibility requirement is met

1,000 Coins = $1.00 USD — fixed and published

To be completely clear: battles do not earn Coins. Winning a battle earns XP and moves your rank. Coins come only from completing offer tasks and from verified referrals. Watching a rewarded ad unlocks extra battles or restores lifelines — it does not earn Coins either.
